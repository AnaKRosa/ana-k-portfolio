Fore Play Indoor Golf
Booking Platform Designed for Conversion

A marketing site and booking web app for an indoor golf venue: bays, food, and accounts in one flow.

Web App Design UI Design Design System
Live Website →
The problem

Fore Play needed more than a marketing site. Players had to book bays, add food, and manage their account, without jumping between separate tools.

The solution

I designed a marketing site and a booking web app on one design system, so a player can go from the home page to checkout, and manage their account, in one flow.

Profile, security, notifications, and wallet

Design System

Foundations, components, and layout & icons

Outcome

I designed the Fore Play marketing site and booking web app as one system: pricing, memberships, and contact pages, plus booking, food ordering, accounts, and login, in desktop and mobile.

Players can book one or several bays, add food to their session, and pay in a single checkout, while managing their profile, security, notifications, and wallet from one account area.
