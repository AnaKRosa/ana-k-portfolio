The problem

Hire South was growing, but its website was not keeping up. The team needed more pages to explain its services, without losing the search traffic the site already had.

The solution

I redesigned and rebuilt the site in Webflow, growing it from 7 to 15 pages, with a simple structure the founders can edit on their own.

Homepage

Key Features

Salary calculator, blog, article template, and talent profiles

Inner Pages

Why LATAM, How We Work, About Us, and a marketing role landing page

Design System

Outcome

I designed and built the new hiresouth.com in Webflow: 7 pages rebuilt and 8 new ones, with a salary calculator, a filtered blog, and talent profiles in the CMS.

The founders edit the site on their own, and it launched on the same URLs. The SEO score went up from 92 to 96.
