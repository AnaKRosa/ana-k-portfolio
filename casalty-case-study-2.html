Casalty Real Estate Website
Designed for Luxury Conversion

A destination-funnel website and brand identity for a Caribbean luxury real estate developer.

UX Strategy Website Design Brand Identity
The problem

Casalty needed more than a beautiful website. Most real estate sites just list properties — this one needed to position the brand as premium and guide buyers toward inquiry.

The solution

I designed a website structured around destination funnels instead of generic listings, on a reusable component system, with the brand identity to match.
