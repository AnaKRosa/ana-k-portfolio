Fore Play Indoor Golf Booking Platform Designed for Conversion

A full product system built to connect marketing, bay booking, and food ordering into one seamless experience — from first visit to checkout.

Product Strategy Web App Design UI Design
Live Website →

The Challenge

Fore Play Indoor Golf needed more than a marketing site. It required a full booking system capable of handling real group behavior, multiple bays, food orders, and accounts, without feeling like three different products stitched together.

Most indoor golf and simulator venues rely on generic third-party booking widgets bolted onto a simple site. This project focused on building a single, connected experience instead.

Key issues to solve
  • No unified system connecting the marketing site to the booking flow
  • Booking a single bay at a time, with no support for groups reserving multiple bays together
  • No way to order food or drinks as part of the same session
  • No structured account experience for memberships, credits, or wallet balances

Objective

Design a strategic, high-converting booking platform that:

  • Organizes pricing, memberships, and gift cards clearly
  • Guides users through booking one or several bays at once
  • Integrates food ordering directly into the booking flow
  • [Objetivo adicional si aplica]

Results

The platform was designed as a complete digital ecosystem — marketing, authentication, booking, and account management working together.

Design

  • Full design system with 40+ components
  • Marketing site — 4 pages, desktop + mobile
  • Complete auth system (login / signup / reset)
  • Account dashboard, V1 and V2

Strategy

  • Multi-bay booking in a single checkout
  • Food ordering cross-sell inside the booking flow

Marketing site, Home

Design System & Components

A modular component library built for scale — every element designed to work independently and together.

Component Strategy

  • Reusable navigation with dropdown variations
  • Green / tan palette rooted in the golf setting
  • Sora semibold type scale for headers
  • Product card system reused across pricing, memberships, and food
  • CTA blocks (primary / secondary variations)
  • Section containers with predefined spacing rules

Each component was designed with variants to allow rapid iteration across booking, account, and marketing screens.

Why it matters
  • Keeps the public site and the logged-in app visually consistent
  • Speeds up new screens without rebuilding components
  • Reduces development friction
  • Gives development a clean, reusable spec to build from

Booking Flow in Action

Fore Play is structured around reserving a bay fast, whether it's one player or a group.

Date + time selection, bay availability, multi-bay cart, checkout

Booking Strategy

Each booking follows a guided sequence:

  1. Pick a date and see live bay availability
  2. Select one bay, or add several to the same cart
  3. Review each bay's time, duration, and price as a separate line
  4. Add food or drinks for the session (optional)
  5. Checkout with subtotal, tax, and total visible throughout

Instead of separating booking and food ordering into two systems, the flow keeps everything in one checkout, reducing steps and drop-off.

Food ordering — integrated cross-sell inside the same checkout
Login, signup, error states, cookie consent, and password reset

UX Decisions

  • Account dashboard reorganized into tabs (V2) to cut scroll in a page with a lot of information
  • Explicit error states on login/signup (short password, existing account, mismatched passwords) to reduce drop-off
  • Cookie consent explored in three variants to balance compliance with minimal friction

Outcome

The final result is a structured booking platform designed to turn a first visit into a completed reservation, with no gaps between marketing, sign up, and checkout.

Instead of functioning as a static marketing site with a separate booking tool bolted on, Fore Play now operates as one connected system. A player can land on the home page, understand pricing and memberships, create an account, reserve one or several bays for their group, and add food to their session, all without leaving the flow.

The project shows how a clear design system, reusable components, and a checkout built around real group behavior (multiple bays, shared carts, food add-ons) can turn a booking flow from a simple form into a tool that actually drives revenue.

Fore Play Indoor Golf is positioned not as another scheduling page, but as a complete digital front door for the business, built to convert first-time visitors into returning players.
